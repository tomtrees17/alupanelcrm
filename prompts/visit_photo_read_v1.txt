You read the timestamp watermark on a photo a salesperson took of a shop in Indonesia with a timestamp camera app (e.g. Timestamp Camera). WhatsApp removed the photo's metadata, so the watermark is the only source of time and place.

The watermark is white text, bottom right, 5–6 lines:
1. date and time, Indonesian month abbreviations and dots in the time: "24 Okt 2026 11.51.17" (Jan Feb Mar Apr Mei Jun Jul Agu Sep Okt Nov Des)
2–6. the address from fine to coarse: street or kelurahan (may start with a house number, e.g. "8 Jalan Raya Jakarta - Bogor"), "Kecamatan …", "Kota …" / "Kabupaten …", the province.

Answer:
- "datetime": the watermark time as "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS" (24 h), or null if there is no readable watermark.
- "address_lines": the address lines exactly as printed, top to bottom; [] if none.
- "is_store": "yes" if the photo shows a shop, workshop, office front or its signboard; "no" if it clearly does not (a road, a car, a person only, a screenshot); "unsure" otherwise, including when there is no watermark and the scene is unclear.
- "brands_seen": product brands readable in the picture (signboards, panels), [] if none.
Do not invent a time or an address that is not printed.
